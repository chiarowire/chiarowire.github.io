Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards are new tools Anthropic added to Claude on November 10, 2026. Sheets is a simple spreadsheet that Claude can build and edit for you. Whiteboards is a shared board for planning with your team. Both are in beta on paid plans, on web, desktop and mobile. Anthropic doesn't list them for the free plan.

## What happened: Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Data

Anthropic [announced](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data) three workplace tools at once, a day after it released its [Claude Fable 5.2 model](../claude-fable-5-2/). Here's what each one does, based on Anthropic's posts and [TechCrunch's report](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/):

- **Claude Sheets (beta).** You describe a spreadsheet, like a budget or a project tracker, and Claude makes it, with tabs, formulas, formatting and drop-down menus. Claude edits cells directly and lists each change it made, so you can check its work. You can share a sheet and work on it with teammates.
- **Claude Whiteboards (beta).** A shared board for brainstorming. Claude can turn a rough plan into a board with a timeline, owners and dates, group sticky notes by theme, and turn the board into a document or slide deck.
- **Claude Data (now out of beta).** This is the renamed Claude Dashboards. It connects to a company's business data and builds charts and dashboards from it. It's aimed at businesses with that data already set up.

Anthropic is also upfront about the limits. It calls what Sheets offers "simple spreadsheet functionality" for now, and points people who need full Excel features to export the file or use its Excel add-in instead.

| | What it does | Status | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Sheets | Builds and edits spreadsheets | Beta | Paid plans |
| Claude Whiteboards | Shared planning boards | Beta | Paid plans |
| Claude Data | Dashboards from business data | Generally available | Paid plans, needs a data connection |
| Claude Meetings | Joins video calls to take notes | Coming soon / early beta | Team and Enterprise |

## What about Claude Meetings?

Claude Meetings lets a team add Claude to a video call as a visible participant. It follows the conversation, takes notes and updates the documents the team is working on.

Reports differ on timing. Anthropic's own [roundup post](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors) lists Meetings as "coming soon." TechCrunch reports it's starting in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, with Google Meet as the only supported app at first. TechCrunch also says Claude announces itself in the call's chat, and won't join calls with people from outside the company unless an admin allows it.

## What it means for you

If you pay for Claude yourself, on a plan like Pro, Sheets and Whiteboards should show up for you as beta features. Anthropic's announcement says all three tools are "available on all paid plans."

If you use Claude through work, it depends on your company. On Team plans the tools are on by default. On Enterprise plans, Sheets and Whiteboards start switched off until an admin turns them on, according to Anthropic.

If you're on the free plan, these tools aren't listed for you.

The bigger change is where Claude does the work. Until now, asking a chatbot for a spreadsheet usually meant getting a file to download and open somewhere else. Now the sheet or board lives inside Claude, on Anthropic's servers, the same way [your cloud photos](../where-are-cloud-photos-stored/) live on someone else's computers. You and your teammates open the same copy.

Anthropic also says apps like Adobe, Atlassian, Box and Notion can now open inside Claude as full apps, next to the conversation, so you don't have to switch tabs.

## Is it worth caring about?

*Our take:* it depends on how you work.

- **If you already use Claude for work**, this matters most to you. Small jobs like a trip budget, a sign-up tracker or a project timeline can now stay in one place.
- **If you live in Excel or Google Sheets**, Anthropic itself says Sheets is basic for now. It isn't trying to replace a full spreadsheet app yet.
- **If you don't use Claude at all**, the trend is the story. AI companies are moving from chat windows into the everyday office tools people use all day. TechCrunch notes OpenAI and Meta have made similar moves. Expect more of this from every big AI company.

One reminder: like any chatbot, Claude can get things wrong, and that includes formulas. Anthropic shows each change Claude makes, which helps, but it's worth checking the numbers before you rely on them.
