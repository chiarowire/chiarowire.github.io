Google is changing how Gemini chat history works. Until now, one setting decided both whether your chats were saved and whether Google could use them to train its AI. Google announced on November 16, 2026 that those will become two separate choices. There's a second change too: with the new History setting off, future chats will be kept for about two months instead of 72 hours.

## What happened to Gemini chat history

Google explained the change in a [post on its official blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/updated-controls/) on November 16. It says it's "giving you more control over when your Gemini chat history is used to train generative AI models."

Before this update, the Gemini app had a single switch called Keep Activity. [TechCrunch reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/google-to-keep-gemini-chats-for-two-months-up-from-72-hours-even-with-history-off/) that this one setting covered both your chat history and AI training. If you wanted Google to stop using your chats to train its models, you also had to give up your saved history.

Now those are split. One control decides whether your chats are saved to your history. A separate control decides whether they can be used to train Google's AI. Google's post says you can still choose how long your history is kept, and review or delete it whenever you like.

The change is rolling out worldwide in the Gemini app "in the coming weeks," Google says, so you may not see it yet.

## The catch: history-off chats are kept longer

Here's the part worth knowing. Google's post says that if you turn the new History setting off, "future chats will be saved for around two months."

That's a big jump. According to TechCrunch, which checked an archived copy of Google's Gemini privacy page from November 12, chats with history off, plus temporary chats, used to be kept for 72 hours.

Google gives two reasons. Its post says those chats are used to respond to you, handle your feedback, and protect Google, its users and the public. It also says it's investing in safety and security "as AI systems get smarter." TechCrunch notes that Google didn't say what prompted the change.

Google also says temporary chats aren't used to train its AI models, according to TechCrunch.

Here's the change at a glance, based on Google's post and TechCrunch's reporting:

| | Before | After the update |
|---|---|---|
| Settings | One switch (Keep Activity) for history and training | Separate controls for history and for AI training |
| Keep history but skip AI training? | Not possible | Possible |
| Chats kept with history off | 72 hours | About two months |
| Rollout | Not applicable | Worldwide, over the coming weeks |

## What it means for you

What changes for you depends on which setting you had before. TechCrunch reports:

- **If you had Keep Activity off**, both new settings start off. Your chats stay out of your history and out of AI training, but they're held for about two months instead of 72 hours.
- **If you had Keep Activity on**, your chat history stays on, and you can now decide separately whether those chats help train Google's AI.

TechCrunch's report doesn't say how this works for work or school accounts. Those are usually managed by an organization's admin, so check with yours if you use Gemini through work.

For comparison, TechCrunch notes that OpenAI may keep temporary ChatGPT chats for up to 30 days, and Anthropic keeps incognito Claude chats for 30 days by default. Google's new window is longer than both. (If you're new to these tools, our explainer on [how ChatGPT-style chatbots work](../how-does-chatgpt-work/) covers why companies want chat data for training in the first place.)

## Is it worth caring about?

*Our take:* yes, if you use Gemini for anything personal.

- **It matters most to people who wanted to keep their history but not train Google's AI.** That combination wasn't possible before, and it will be once the update reaches you.
- **It matters to people who switched history off for privacy.** Your chats won't show up in your history, but Google will now hold them for about two months rather than three days. That's worth knowing when you decide what to type.
- **The bigger picture:** Gemini keeps showing up in more places, from [your car](../gemini-carplay/) to [smart glasses](../google-gemini-smart-glasses/), Google keeps adding new models like [Gemini 4 Neon](../gemini-4-neon/), and AI assistants are starting to [handle purchases](../chatgpt-wallet/) too. The more you talk to an assistant, the more these settings matter, especially now that Google's AI Mode can [connect to Wallet and Drive](../google-ai-mode-wallet-drive/). Like [your cloud photos](../where-are-cloud-photos-stored/), your chats are stored on the company's servers, not just on your phone.

Google's [blog post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/updated-controls/) links to its help page for the new settings. Once the update reaches you, they'll be in the Gemini app's settings.
