Google's Gemini smart glasses are now up for pre-order. Google announced on November 12, 2026 that its "intelligent eyewear," designed with Warby Parker and Gentle Monster and built by Samsung, goes on sale December 10 in the US and South Korea. Prices start at $395, and the glasses work with recent Android phones and iPhones.

## What happened: Google opens pre-orders for Gemini glasses

Google shared the details in a [post on its official blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/xr-ar/google-intelligent-eyewear-collections-pre-order). Samsung first showed the glasses at its Galaxy Unpacked event in London back in July, in a [Samsung newsroom post](https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-brings-galaxy-ecosystem-into-everyday-eyewear) that described them as built with Google and running Google's Gemini assistant. What's new this week is the part most people wait for: styles, prices and a date.

Pre-orders are open online and in stores at Warby Parker and Gentle Monster, and Google says you can try a pair at some Warby Parker, Gentle Monster and Google Store locations. Here are the two collections:

| | Warby Parker | Gentle Monster |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | $395 | $449 |
| Styles | 3 (Alden, Reid, Wren) | 6 |
| Lenses | Optical or sun | Tinted, clear, blue or brown |
| Prescription | Not stated | Most frames |
| On sale | December 10 | December 10 |

Google says you need a Google account from the US or South Korea, and you need to be 18 or older.

## What can the Gemini glasses do?

Think of them as a pair of normal-looking glasses with a camera, microphones and small speakers built in, connected to your phone. You say "Hey Gemini" or tap the frame, then talk to Google's AI assistant. (If you want the background on how that kind of AI works, see our explainer on [how ChatGPT-style chatbots work](../how-does-chatgpt-work/).)

From Google's list, the main features are:

- **Ask about what you see.** A 12-megapixel camera lets Gemini look at what's in front of you when you ask.
- **Directions.** Google Maps gives turn-by-turn directions through the speakers.
- **Live translation.** Google Translate turns speech in more than 70 languages into your language as people talk.
- **Calls, texts and music.** Take calls, send messages, and play music or audiobooks from apps including YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music and Audible.
- **Accessibility.** A feature called Guided Vision describes your surroundings out loud for people who are blind or have low vision. Google notes it is not a medical device or mobility aid.

Google says the glasses weigh about 41 grams, last up to nine hours on a charge depending on use, and come with a case that can recharge them up to seven times.

## Which phones work with them?

Google lists three groups of phones:

- **Google Pixel:** Pixel 7 series or later, on Android 17 or later.
- **Samsung Galaxy:** Galaxy S, S FE, Z Fold, Z Flip and Fold Ultra models on One UI 9.0 or higher.
- **iPhone:** iPhone 13 Pro or later.

One catch for iPhone owners: Google says some features, including Guided Vision and Audible, work only with an Android phone at launch.

## What about privacy?

A camera on your face raises fair questions, both for you and for the people around you. Google makes several promises in its post. These are the company's claims:

- What the camera shows Gemini isn't saved to your Gemini history, and isn't used to train AI models or target ads.
- Bystanders' faces are automatically redacted (hidden) before processing.
- A light shows when the glasses are taking photos or video, and Google says recording stops if that light is covered.
- Each pair has an off switch.

## What it means for you

**If you already use Gemini on your phone**, these glasses move the same assistant off the screen and onto your face. It's the same direction as Google's recent push to put [Gemini on Apple CarPlay](../gemini-carplay/): AI you talk to, in places where pulling out a phone is awkward.

**If you wear glasses anyway**, the prescription options are the key detail. Google says most Gentle Monster frames offer prescription lenses, and the Warby Parker styles come in optical versions.

**If you live outside the US or South Korea**, nothing changes yet. Google hasn't announced other countries.

## Is the Gemini smart glasses launch worth caring about?

*Our take:* yes, as a sign of where AI assistants are heading, even if you never wear a pair.

- **It matters most to people who already lean on Gemini or Google Maps and Translate**, and to anyone who wants hands-free help while walking, traveling or cooking.
- **It matters less if you're happy with your phone**, or if a camera on your glasses doesn't sit right with you or the people around you.
- **The bigger picture:** the big tech companies all want their AI assistant to be the one you talk to all day. Apple is reworking Siri (see our [Siri AI waitlist](../siri-ai-waitlist/) post), and Google is putting Gemini in cars and now in glasses. Where that assistant lives is turning into the real contest.

## What's next

Google says the glasses go on sale in stores and online on December 10, 2026, in the US and South Korea. It hasn't named other countries or dates.
