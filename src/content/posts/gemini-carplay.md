Gemini is now on Apple CarPlay. Google announced on November 11, 2026 that Gemini Live, the voice-chat mode of its Gemini app, is rolling out worldwide to iPhone users. Connect your phone to a CarPlay car, tap the Gemini icon on the car's screen, and you can talk with it out loud while you drive.

## What happened: Gemini comes to Apple CarPlay

Google shared the news in a [post on its official blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-on-apple-carplay/). The company says Gemini on CarPlay is rolling out "globally starting today to all Gemini users on iOS," in every language Gemini Live already supports. Google had already brought Gemini to Android Auto and to cars with Google built in, so this fills the iPhone gap.

CarPlay is Apple's system that puts a simplified version of your iPhone on your car's dashboard screen. The Gemini app now shows up there as its own icon. Google lists three things you need before it appears:

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| The latest Gemini app on your iPhone | The CarPlay support comes in an app update |
| Microphone permission turned on | It's a voice-first feature |
| At least one Gemini Live chat on your phone first | Google says this is needed before it works in the car |

## What can you ask Gemini in the car?

Gemini Live is the mode where you speak to the AI and it speaks back, like a phone call rather than typing into a chat box. (If you're curious how the chatbot behind it works, see our explainer on [how ChatGPT-style AI works](../how-does-chatgpt-work/).)

Google's examples for the car fall into a few groups:

- **Your day.** Ask what's on your calendar, or get help preparing for a meeting.
- **Your inbox.** If you've connected Gmail and Google Calendar, Gemini can catch you up on unread emails and summarize long threads out loud. Google's daily brief feature has to be switched on first, and Google says some personal features, including the daily brief, aren't available to users under 18 or in the European Economic Area and Nigeria.
- **Places.** Ask for a lunch spot or background on a landmark you're passing.
- **Thinking out loud.** Rehearse a presentation or talk through a problem.

Google says your car chats sync with the Gemini app on your iPhone, so the conversation is there when you park. Like [your cloud photos](../where-are-cloud-photos-stored/), that history lives on the company's servers, not just on your phone.

## What it means for you

**If you use an iPhone with CarPlay and already use Gemini**, this is the main change: Gemini's voice mode now works on your car's screen, not just on your phone.

**If you rely on Siri in the car**, nothing changes. Google's post doesn't mention Siri at all. Gemini appears as a separate app you tap to open. Apple has been rolling out its own upgraded assistant, which we covered in our post on the [Siri AI waitlist](../siri-ai-waitlist/).

**If you're not a Gemini user**, there's nothing to set up and nothing new on your dashboard.

Google pitches the feature around driving. It describes Gemini as a "conversational driving companion that minimizes distraction," and says you can interrupt it or pause the chat when you need to focus on the road. Those are Google's claims. It hasn't published any testing to back them up.

## Is it worth caring about?

*Our take:* for most people, it's a small convenience, not a big change.

- **It matters most to commuters who already use Gemini**, especially people on Google's email and calendar apps. Hearing a summary of your morning without touching your phone is the clearest use.
- **It matters less if you're happy with Siri**, or if you don't use Google's apps for email and calendar. Much of what Google shows off depends on those connections.
- **The bigger trend:** AI chatbots are moving off the phone screen and into places where talking beats typing, like the car. Google putting its assistant on Apple's dashboard is one more step in that direction. ## What's next

Google says the rollout started on November 11 and covers all Gemini users on iOS. Its post doesn't name a date by which everyone will have it.
