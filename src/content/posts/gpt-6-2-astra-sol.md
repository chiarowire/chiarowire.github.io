GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol are two new AI models OpenAI released on November 17, 2026. Astra is the heavyweight for big, complicated projects. Sol is the faster everyday model. Paying ChatGPT users on Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu can pick them in ChatGPT Work and Codex. OpenAI hasn't said anything about free users.

## What happened with GPT-6.2

OpenAI announced both models in a [post on its own site](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol), and its [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes) list them under November 17. They're updates to OpenAI's earlier GPT-6 Astra and Sol models. OpenAI describes their jobs like this:

| Model | What OpenAI says it's for |
|---|---|
| GPT-6.2 Astra | Its frontier model for "complex professional work": documents, spreadsheets, slides, coding |
| GPT-6.2 Sol | Its "go-to model for everyday work," built to be quick and efficient |

In plain words, Astra is the one that thinks longer about hard jobs, and Sol is the one most people will reach for day to day. (If "model" is a fuzzy term, our explainer on [how ChatGPT works](../how-does-chatgpt-work/) covers what's actually going on inside.)

## Where you'll find them in ChatGPT

This is the part that trips people up, so here it is plainly:

- **Who gets them:** OpenAI says the models are available to "all Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users." Free users aren't mentioned in either OpenAI page we read.
- **Where:** in **ChatGPT Work** (you pick a model from the Work model picker) and in **Codex**, OpenAI's coding tool. The release notes call them a "preview" there.
- **The regular chat isn't changing yet:** OpenAI's release notes say the normal Chat tab "keeps its existing model choices." So if you only use ChatGPT for quick back-and-forth questions, you may not see GPT-6.2 at all for now. (Update: OpenAI is now [merging the Chat and Work tabs](../chatgpt-chat-work-merge/) into one.)
- **Faster replies for some plans:** OpenAI also mentions a speed option called Ultrafast for some Pro subscribers, with Enterprise and Edu workspaces able to turn it on. OpenAI's post doesn't explain it in detail.

## What GPT-6.2 means for you

OpenAI's pitch for GPT-6.2 Sol is that it's "smarter, more efficient," and lets you "complete more tasks on the same paid plan." Put simply: if you pay for ChatGPT and keep running into usage limits, OpenAI says Sol should stretch those limits further, because it does the same work with less computing.

For Astra, OpenAI says the documents, spreadsheets and slides it makes follow professional conventions more closely, with less filler. That lines up with where the big AI companies are all heading: chatbots that hand you a finished file, not just a chat reply. Anthropic made a similar move when it added [Sheets and Whiteboards to Claude](../claude-sheets-whiteboards/).

OpenAI also tested Sol on ordinary life chores. Its examples include shortlisting kindergartens after a move and planning meals for a parent with a new diagnosis. OpenAI says Sol beat the older, bigger GPT-6 Astra on those tasks at about one-seventh of the cost. Those are OpenAI's own tests of its own model, and we haven't seen anyone else check them, so treat them as OpenAI's claim and not a guarantee. Any AI can still get things wrong, so double-check anything important.

## A note on safety

OpenAI says both models came with "tighter safeguards" for risky cybersecurity requests. It also says this could sometimes block legitimate security work. For most people, that just means ChatGPT may refuse some hacking-related questions more often. OpenAI doesn't describe any change for everyday requests.

## Is GPT-6.2 worth caring about?

*Our take:* it depends on how you use ChatGPT.

- **If you pay for ChatGPT and use Work or Codex,** this is a new option in your model menu, and OpenAI says it gets more done within the same limits. That makes it worth knowing about.
- **If you mostly use the regular Chat tab or the free version,** nothing in OpenAI's announcement changes for you today.
- **If you use other chatbots,** the bigger picture is a familiar one. Every few weeks, one of the big companies ships a model that's a bit better and a bit cheaper to run. Google just did the same with [Gemini 4 Neon](../gemini-4-neon/), and Anthropic did it with [Claude Fable 5.2](../claude-fable-5-2/). That competition is how better AI tends to reach more people over time.

OpenAI has been busy in ChatGPT generally, too. Just days before this, it started letting AI agents [shop for you with ChatGPT Wallet](../chatgpt-wallet/).
