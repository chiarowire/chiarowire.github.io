Apple's M6 MacBook Pro and M6 iMac are here. The 14-inch MacBook Pro starts at $1,999 and the 24-inch iMac at $1,499. Apple announced both on October 27, 2026. Pre-orders opened October 28, and both arrive November 6. Against last year's M5, it's a normal yearly step. Against a Mac that's several years old, it's a big jump.

Here's what changed, how to read Apple's "up to" numbers, and what comes next.

## What happened

Apple announced the two Macs in separate press releases on October 27 ([MacBook Pro](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-introduces-the-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6/), [iMac](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-new-imac-with-m6/)). Both use M6, the chip Apple first put in the Mac mini on August 25. Apple calls it its "first state-of-the-art 2-nanometer chip" ([Apple, August 2026](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/)).

Inside M6, according to Apple:

- **A 12-core CPU** (the main processor), with three sizes of core: 2 "super" cores for the fastest single jobs, 4 performance cores and 6 efficiency cores for light background work.
- **A 12-core GPU** (the graphics processor), two more cores than M5, with a small AI accelerator built into each core.
- **A dual 16-core Neural Engine**, Apple's dedicated AI hardware.
- **Up to 170GB/s of memory bandwidth**, meaning how fast data moves between the chip and its memory. That's up from 153GB/s on M5 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/apple-unveils-new-14-inch-macbook-pro-with-m6-chip/)). The catch: only the 24GB and 32GB versions get 170GB/s. The 16GB base model stays at 153GB/s, per Apple's spec sheet ([MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/10/27/m6-14-inch-macbook-pro-memory-bandwidth-split/)).

The MacBook Pro's design, display and ports carry over from the M5 model. The main upgrades are the chip, faster wireless (Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6), and what Apple calls the longest battery life ever in a Mac. The iMac also keeps its design. Its 12-core models add 2.5-gigabit Ethernet, and it goes straight from M4 to M6, skipping M5 entirely ([TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2026/10/27/m6-comes-to-the-14-inch-macbook-pro-and-imac/)).

## Why it matters

### What "up to 30% faster" really means

Apple says the MacBook Pro has up to 30% faster graphics and up to 25% faster on-device AI than the M5 model. Apple's footnotes show what that's measured against: a 14-inch MacBook Pro with M5, in tests Apple ran itself. "Up to" means best case.

The eye-popping numbers, like "up to 13x faster" AI, are measured against a 13-inch MacBook Pro with the original M1 chip. That's fair if you're still on an M1, but it's a very different comparison from last year's model. The iMac's "up to 2x faster graphics" is measured against the M4 iMac it replaces.

### What independent tests found

Since the same M6 chip has been in the Mac mini since August, reviewers have already tested it. Jason Snell at Six Colors measured M6 about **11% faster** than M5 on single-core CPU work, **23%** on multi-core and **24%** on graphics, and called it an average generational improvement ([Six Colors](https://sixcolors.com/post/2026/09/m6-mac-mini-review-tiny-pricey-and-powerful/)). So Apple's graphics claim lands near the real-world range, while everyday single tasks are closer to 10%.

### What "2 nanometers" means

"2nm" sounds like a measurement. It isn't. Chip-process names stopped matching any real size on the chip decades ago, and they now work more like model years. We break that down in [what 3nm (and 2nm) really means](../what-does-3nm-mean/). A newer generation usually packs transistors more densely, and here Apple used that to add cores and stretch battery life.

### Who it's for

If you're on an Intel Mac or an M1, these are big upgrades, and that's exactly who Apple's biggest numbers are aimed at. If you have an M4 or M5 Mac that does what you need, M6 alone isn't a reason to switch. TidBITS' Adam Engst reached a similar conclusion. (This is how to read the specs, not a buying recommendation.)

### Is there a 16-inch M6 MacBook Pro?

No. One day later, on October 28, Apple announced a separate MacBook Pro with an OLED "PROLED XDR" display and touch, a first for the Mac. It comes in 14- and 16-inch sizes and runs **M5 Pro and M5 Max**, not M6, starting at $2,899 ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/)). So right now Apple's newest chip isn't in its top laptop. The M6 model is the entry Pro.

## What's next

- **November 6:** both M6 Macs arrive in Apple Stores and at authorized resellers. Pre-orders are open in 35 countries and regions, including the US.
- **Software:** they ship with macOS 27. Apple notes that its new Siri AI is a beta in macOS 27.2, in a limited set of languages.
- **More M6 Macs?** Apple hasn't announced an M6 MacBook Air or M6 Pro and Max chips. Expect rumors. We'll cover them when Apple confirms something.
