The Siri AI waitlist is mostly gone. Apple has updated its support page so that turning on Siri AI in iOS 27 now starts the download right away, instead of putting everyone in a queue first. Apple says a waitlist can still return "during times of high demand." Siri AI is still labeled a beta.

## What happened to the Siri AI waitlist

When Apple released Siri AI in September, switching it on put you on a waitlist, and you waited until Apple let you in. An archived version of Apple's support page said enabling it "will add you to a waitlist," according to [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/05/siri-ai-waitlist-limited-times-high-demand-apple/), which says the page was updated on November 2. 9to5Mac first spotted the change.

Apple's current page, [How to get Siri AI](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893), now says that after you set it up, "Siri AI will begin to download automatically." The waitlist only comes back when lots of people are trying at once: "During times of high demand, you might be added to a waitlist first, and wait times can vary."

Turning it on works the same as before, per Apple: open **Settings**, tap **Siri**, then choose **Try Siri AI (Beta)**. You'll pick a voice, do a short voice setup and review the privacy settings.

## What it means for you

If you have a phone, tablet or computer that supports it, you can probably get Siri AI today without the wait. Here's who can, based on Apple's support page:

| Device | Needs |
|---|---|
| iPhone | iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or any iPhone 16 or newer, on iOS 27 |
| iPad | iPad with M1 or newer, or iPad mini with A17 Pro or newer, on iPadOS 27 |
| Mac | Mac with M1 or newer, or MacBook Neo, on macOS 27 |
| Apple Watch | Series 9 or newer, Ultra 2 or newer, or SE 3, paired with an iPhone that has Siri AI on |
| Apple Vision Pro | Any model, on visionOS 27 |

*Source: [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893). Check the page for the full list.*

There are a few catches, all from that same page:

- **Language:** your device and Siri need to be set to the same supported language. Right now that's English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese or Spanish.
- **Where you live:** your Apple Account region has to be eligible. In the EU, Siri AI isn't available on iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch, only on Mac and Vision Pro. It doesn't currently work with a mainland China account.
- **Age:** it isn't available to users under 13.
- **Daily limits:** features that run on Apple's servers, Siri AI included, have daily usage limits that Apple says may change.

That last one is worth a sentence of explanation. Apple's page lists Siri AI among features that rely on models running on Apple's own servers, much like a chatbot such as ChatGPT ([here's how those work](../how-does-chatgpt-work/)). Those servers cost money to run, and a waitlist is one way to stop too many people arriving at once. MacRumors suggests Apple spent the two months since launch watching that load before relaxing the queue. Apple hasn't said why it made the change.

## Is it worth caring about?

*Our take:* if you've been waiting in the queue, or held off turning Siri AI on because of it, this matters to you. It's the difference between "sometime later" and "now." If you don't have a supported device, this change doesn't help yet. Notice it's all recent hardware. For why newer chips keep getting more capable, see [what "3nm" really means](../what-does-3nm-mean/). It's also why Apple pitches its new [M6 Macs](../m6-macbook-pro-imac/) on faster on-device AI.

It's also still a beta. Apple's own label says so, which means it can change, and some things may not work perfectly yet.

## What's next

Apple has already said one more change is coming. Its support page says "expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future." Apple hasn't announced a price, a date or what the paid tier would include, so for now the daily limits are the only limits.

The waitlist itself hasn't been removed for good either. If demand spikes, Apple says you may still be put in a queue. Siri AI is also the centerpiece of Apple's new smart display, [Apple HomeView](../apple-homeview/), which arrives November 13. And Siri isn't the only AI assistant on your iPhone's car screen now: Google has brought [Gemini to Apple CarPlay](../gemini-carplay/).
