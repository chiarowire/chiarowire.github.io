The ChatGPT Chat and Work merge is starting. OpenAI is combining the two tabs into one ChatGPT experience, and from December, paid plans will draw on one shared usage allowance across ChatGPT and Codex. OpenAI says most people won't notice a difference, quick Instant chats won't count against the allowance, and a small group of heavy users may hit their limits sooner.

## What happened with ChatGPT Chat and Work

Right now, ChatGPT has two separate places to work, especially in the desktop app. **Chat** is the familiar back-and-forth conversation. **Work** is the side built for bigger jobs: you hand it files and plugins and it builds things like a website or a slide deck, or does tasks on the web while you watch and steer.

Here's how the change has unfolded:

| When | What happened |
|---|---|
| Early November | OpenAI emailed users that Chat and Work would merge into one experience, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/openai-is-finally-merging-chatgpts-chat-and-work-tabs/). Codex, its coding tool, stays separate in the desktop app. |
| November 25 | The merged version began reaching a small group of Pro subscribers, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/), citing a post by an OpenAI product lead. |
| December | OpenAI's [Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625) says usage for Plus, Pro and Business plans becomes shared across ChatGPT and Codex. |

In the email TechCrunch quoted, OpenAI said the merge lets you go from "exploring an idea to having ChatGPT carry it through, all in one place."

## How the shared ChatGPT usage limits work

This is the part that affects paying users most. According to OpenAI's [help page on usage limits](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625):

- **One pool instead of several.** From December, your plan's allowance covers ChatGPT and Codex together, instead of each having its own.
- **It's measured in tokens.** Tokens are the small chunks of text an AI reads and writes (our [ChatGPT explainer](../how-does-chatgpt-work/) covers them). Bigger, smarter models and harder tasks use more.
- **Instant chat doesn't count.** OpenAI says Instant chat "will not draw from your usage," so quick everyday questions on that model stay outside the pool.
- **You can see where you stand.** Settings → Usage shows your combined remaining usage and when it resets.
- **When you run out,** OpenAI lists three options: wait for the reset, change plans, or buy credits for eligible features.

The help page also says a small group may see the change before December.

## What it means for you

- **If you use free ChatGPT:** neither OpenAI's help page nor the TechCrunch reports mention free accounts, so we can't say anything changes for you.
- **If you pay and mostly ask quick questions:** OpenAI says most people won't wait for resets any more often than they do now, and Instant chats don't count anyway.
- **If you pay and lean hard on the heavy stuff,** like long Work projects, the most advanced models and Codex all in the same week: OpenAI says a small share of heavy users may hit the shared limit sooner than before, because everything now comes from one pool.
- **If the two tabs confused you,** this should make ChatGPT simpler. There'll be one place to ask a question or hand off a bigger task.

This lands right after OpenAI's new models. [GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](../gpt-6-2-astra-sol/) arrived in Work first and began rolling out to the Chat tab on November 23, according to OpenAI's [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes). Once the tabs merge, that split stops mattering.

## Is the ChatGPT Chat and Work merge worth caring about?

*Our take:* it matters most to people who pay for ChatGPT and use it a lot.

For casual users, this is mostly a tidier app. For heavy paid users, the thing to know is that limits will work differently from December. One shared pool is simpler to track, but it also means a long Codex session and a big Work project draw from the same allowance. Checking Settings → Usage is the easiest way to see how that plays out for you.

The bigger picture: chatbots are turning from question-and-answer tools into assistants that carry out whole tasks. Anthropic added [Sheets and Whiteboards to Claude](../claude-sheets-whiteboards/), Google is wiring [Wallet and Drive into AI Mode](../google-ai-mode-wallet-drive/), and OpenAI's agents can now [shop with ChatGPT Wallet](../chatgpt-wallet/). Folding Work into the main ChatGPT screen is OpenAI putting that "do it for me" side front and center.
